# Surface: portfolio home (single page)

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters screening an early-career fullstack engineer (first), freelance clients (second). Action: email Karl or open the resume.
Proof: four featured works from the resume (Prestige Pickleball Society, Admissions Chatbot, Wingman, SAMAHAN Palaro), seven other works, experience, education. No invented metrics, quotes, or clients.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an architecture practice monograph: works documented as plates with a data column of recorded facts. Refuses the developer-portfolio default of hero + equal card grid + modal.
OWN-WORLD: Cool uncoated paper (#f1f1ee), near-black ink, graphite secondary, hairline rules, one ultramarine spot used only for the active index mark, links, and the primary action. Schibsted Grotesk for name, titles, and data; Source Serif 4 for notes. Tabular figures. No cards, shadows, chips, or tracked eyebrow labels.
STORY: The visitor sees who Karl is and his strongest built work within one viewport, reads the facts of each work, then contacts him or opens the resume.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Calling Card cover (surface round, dealt lead). Identity set large on the left: name at up to 6rem over two lines, role, motto, Email Karl and Resume (PDF), then the education and location lines. On the right, the four featured works as a 2x2 of small 16:9 top-cropped screenshot thumbnails with title and year, then one ruled line of Other work, Experience, Contact. Composition vertically centered. Phones: identity, then a list of thumbnail rows.
FORM: Architect's monograph, candidate 1 of 7 (taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK). Signature interaction: choosing a work opens the book. The cover fades out, the chosen thumbnail alone flies and grows into that work's plate, and the book fades up beneath it, landing on the chosen work. Inside, the index mark slides to the work in view. Back or the name returns to the cover. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
